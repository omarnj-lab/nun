# Sharia review log

Every religious-content list used by Nūn is signed off here by the Sharia reviewer (👳) before any of it is
shown to users. Entries stay `status: draft` / `reviewed_by: null` in the data files until signed.

## M1 · drafts ready for review (2026-09-27)

Generated by `make lists` (`scripts/build_lists.py`) from the verified corpus. **Machine-checked:** Quran
occurrences of every Name and phrase, and the Quran reference of every DuwatBench "quranic" image.
**Not verified (the reviewer's job):** hadith references and gradings, name meanings, and the enumeration itself.

### 1. `data/lists/names.draft.jsonl` · 100 entries (الله + 99)
- The enumeration is the commonly printed list appended to the hadith in Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī (3507). The hadith
  «إن لله تسعة وتسعين اسمًا» is in al-Bukhārī and Muslim **without** a list, and many scholars hold the
  enumeration to be a narrator's addition.
  **Decide:** present it as "the commonly known list", or use a list derived from the Quran and authentic Sunnah?
- **59/100 occur verbatim (with ال) in the Quran.** 41 do not, including القابض الباسط الخافض الرافع
  المذل الواجد الماجد المقدم المؤخر المنتقم الضار النافع المانع الباقي الصبور and others (see the
  `quran_as_written.count = 0` rows). `quran_without_al` counts are plain word matches (e.g. «أحد» = "anyone"),
  **not** evidence of a Name.
- `meaning` is empty for every entry. It must come from an approved source (Jamhara dictionary or the
  reviewer). It is never generated.
- Check spelling and order against the Tirmidhī edition. الأحد is included after الواحد, as in the common
  printed list.

### 2. `data/lists/dhikr.draft.jsonl` · 32 common inscriptions
- **14 occur verbatim in the Quran** (`quran_verbatim_refs`), e.g. «حسبنا الله ونعم الوكيل» 3:173,
  «إنا لله وإنا إليه راجعون» 2:156, «ما شاء الله لا قوة إلا بالله» 18:39.
  **Decide per phrase:** when such a phrase is photographed alone, show it as a verse (with its reference), as a
  dhikr, or both?
- «الله أكبر» matches inside 9:72, 29:45 and 40:10 («ولذكر الله أكبر» …) only as part of longer sentences. As an
  inscription it is the takbīr, not those verses. Same question for «سبحان الله» and «الحمد لله».
- `hadith_candidate` (6 entries) is **from memory, unverified**: al-Bukhārī 6403, 6406, 4205, 3370; Muslim 2691,
  2137. Each needs the book, number and grading confirmed on dorar.net (`dorar_search` link given) or replaced.
  Rule: **no hadith is shown without a source + approved grading.** The other entries have no candidate.
- Add or remove phrases as the reviewer sees fit (common mosque/museum inscriptions we missed).

### 3. `data/lists/inscriptions.draft.yaml` · 184 Quran spans
- 22 canonical spans from SPEC §6.1 (+ the Basmala alone), plus the verse spans of 529 of DuwatBench's 567
  "quranic" images (142 exact, 33 exact but occurring at several places, 9 fuzzy ≥ 90).
- **Ambiguous spans** (`exact-ambiguous`, `also_occurs_at`): e.g. «إن الله على كل شيء قدير» appears at 11 places.
  The app will show *all* references for such fragments, not pick one. Confirm this is the desired behaviour.
- **38 DuwatBench images labelled "quranic" are not verse text:** 35 surah titles or book covers
  («سورة الكهف», «القرآن الكريم»), 1 poetry title («ديوان الحماسة»), 2 × «لا حول ولا قوة إلا بالله» (a dhikr;
  18:39 has only «لا قوة إلا بالله»). They are listed under `unmatched_duwatbench` and will be relabelled for
  training.
- **Coverage:** SPEC targets ~500 spans; 184 exist. **Decide:** which further verses commonly appear in mosques
  and museums should be added (e.g. 2:127, 2:201, 3:8, 3:190, 7:23, 9:18, 17:24, 21:87, 25:74, 59:22–24, the
  endings of al-Baqarah and al-Ḥashr …)? The reviewer or team chooses; I have not added any.
